# Business and Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Company Overview
SoplexAI SMC (Private) Limited provides AI automation, AI agents, custom software development, web and mobile development, and healthcare digital transformation services.

## Target Market
The initial target region is the United States (US).

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Digital marketing agencies
- Software development companies
- Technology-focused businesses

## Business Objective
Orbit aims to identify, research, qualify, score, and prioritize potential customers for SoplexAI.

## Potential Lead Sources
- Public business directories
- Official company websites
- Permitted publicly available business information

## Outreach Channels
Potential channels include email, LinkedIn, and Upwork, subject to platform rules and applicable laws.

## Lead Information
Potential fields include company name, website, industry, location, contact details where lawfully available, source, qualification status, lead score, and research notes.

## Human Review
Outreach messages should require human approval before sending during the initial version.
